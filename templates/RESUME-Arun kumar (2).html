The Journey

From power circuits to data pipelines — a career shaped by discipline, curiosity, and scale.

2004 – 2007 · Foundation
Electrical Engineer
INEL Power System Engineers
Before SAP, before analytics, there were motors, meters, and control panels. Commissioning variable frequency drives and testing power centers taught me that precision isn't optional — it's safety-critical. That discipline carried with me when I moved from power to data.
2007 – 2014 · Transition
SAP BI Consultant
Bristlecone · Deloitte
A leap from power systems to data systems. At Bristlecone and Deloitte, I built CRM-BW implementations and enterprise BI solutions. I built data pipelines the same way I built circuits: methodically, with deep attention to what flows through them.
2014 – Present · Architecture
Solution Architect — Analytics
Itelligence · Accenture (via Navisite)
Today I architect SAP analytics for Fortune 500s — Disney, John Deere, Barry Callebaut. I lead teams across continents, design embedded analytics in S/4HANA, and build SAC dashboards that drive business decisions. The engineer never left; he just works at a different scale.
