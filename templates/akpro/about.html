Our Story

Started With a Simple Belief

AK Projections was born from a simple observation: small businesses deserve the same caliber of web design as Fortune 500 companies, but at a price point that makes sense for them.

We saw restaurant owners struggling with outdated websites, coaches losing clients because of unprofessional online presence, and startups burning through budgets on agencies that took months to deliver.

So we built a better way. Premium design templates combined with rapid customization — giving businesses a stunning online presence in days, not months.
