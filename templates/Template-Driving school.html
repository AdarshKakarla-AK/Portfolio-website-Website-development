Every student matters. Every lesson is tailored. We take pride in building drivers, not just test-passers.

Sri Mathru Driving School is a student-first driving institute located in the heart of Vidyaranyapura, Bengaluru.

Our approach is simple: patient instructors, structured curriculum, and real-world traffic training. Whether you're a complete beginner or brushing up your skills, we meet you where you are.

With 15+ hours of hands-on training and flexible scheduling, we've helped over 900 students become confident, independent drivers.
