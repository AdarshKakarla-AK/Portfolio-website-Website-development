About

The Person Behind the Silicon

Two decades of solving hard problems with microcontrollers, one customer at a time.

I was born in a small town in Andhra Pradesh, where the nearest electronics store was a bus ride away. But I knew by the time I was 16 — I wanted to build things that run on code and current.

From my first job at Dragon Technology in Hyderabad supporting GE and Whirlpool, through nearly a decade at Renesas winning corporate Gold Awards, to leading FAE teams across all of India — every step has been about one thing: understanding a customer's problem and finding the right silicon + software to solve it.

“I still get excited when I see a waveform come to life on a scope.”
